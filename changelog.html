What's New

Every update to Receipts for YNAB, from launch to today. Built by a solo developer and fellow YNAB budgeter, shaped by user feedback.

v1.1.5 September 27, 2026 Latest

Deletion you can trust, and scanning ready for iOS 27.

  • Fixed Delete All Data finishes cleanly: in some libraries, a leftover receipt photo could make it report a failure after it had already signed you out. The wipe now clears those leftovers and finishes cleanly.
  • Fixed A failed deletion tells the truth: if a deletion does fail, the receipts list and the alert now agree that your data is still there.
  • Fixed Reinstalling asks you to sign in: reinstalling the app asks you to sign in to YNAB again, instead of silently reconnecting to the previous account.
  • Fixed Long receipts on iOS 27: long receipts that could fail after a long wait now scan properly, and totals are read more reliably.
  • Improved Clear messages on iOS 27: a receipt too large for the on-device model says so and points you to Manual Entry. If the model is busy, the app asks you to try again later.
  • Fixed Retrying a deletion on iOS 27: if a deletion fails and you retry it, the app may ask you to quit and reopen before trying again. Nothing is removed when that happens.
  • Fixed Unsaved line items: a line item you typed but did not save is refused and highlighted, not silently dropped. A $0.00 item is shown and refused the same way.
  • Fixed Cleaner uploads to YNAB: line breaks in a merchant or item name no longer reach YNAB, and a very long item name is capped at YNAB's memo limit instead of failing the upload.
  • Fixed No false budget warning: the app no longer warns that your budget was deleted every time you return to it.
  • Improved Accessibility: VoiceOver reads totals in your local currency and names the account each toggle controls, screens reflow at the largest text sizes, and small controls meet the 44pt tap target.
  • Improved Privacy: the privacy cover hides your receipts in the app switcher on every screen.
  • Improved Reliability: improvements under the hood.
v1.1.4 August 26, 2026

Truthful results: the app now does what it tells you it did.

  • Fixed Delete All Data clears your receipt photographs: receipt images stored outside the main database were being left behind while deletion reported success. They are removed now.
  • Improved Honest wording on deletion: the old text promised device-level erasure the app cannot reach. Delete All Data clears the app's own data, and anything already uploaded stays in your YNAB account. The wording says so now.
  • Fixed Honest sign-out: if the app cannot confirm your YNAB credential was removed, it says so and refuses to use it, instead of reporting a success it did not earn.
  • Fixed Nothing lingers between sessions: a reply from YNAB that arrives after you sign out can no longer refill accounts and categories behind your new session, and the receipt-link backfill stops and offers Reconnect when your session ends, instead of reporting every remaining receipt as failed.
  • Improved Security: the offline cache no longer stores account balances or category amounts, figures the app never displayed. Existing cache files are rewritten on first launch after the update.
  • Fixed Bank-matched receipts stay in sync: a receipt your bank matches keeps the bank's date and keeps syncing, instead of quietly dropping out.
  • Fixed Conflicting edits: editing the same receipt in two places now tells you about the conflict, rather than letting one change overwrite the other.
  • Improved Accessibility: bigger hit targets on Change Account and Save Locally, Reduce Motion respected in more places, VoiceOver now speaks the uncategorized state on a line item, and the Settings row states your YNAB connection status instead of only tinting it.
  • Fixed Smaller fixes: a $0.00 line item is refused with the reason shown, quantity steps to the next whole unit, a blank merchant names the field instead of showing a storage error, and the sync sheet shows account names rather than raw identifiers.
  • New App icon: a new icon for the app.
  • Improved Reliability: improvements under the hood.
v1.1.3 July 31, 2026

An honest YNAB connection, and storage that is ready for iOS 27.

  • Fixed Accurate connection status: YNAB sign-in sessions end routinely by design. When that happens, the app now tells you and offers to sign back in, instead of showing a connected account while uploads and syncs quietly fail. This includes tokens revoked by YNAB.
  • Improved A clear way back: after a session ends, your receipts, budgets, and account names stay viewable offline, and signing back in restores everything, including the account picker.
  • Improved Ready for iOS 27: receipt line items are now stored in a future-proof format with stable ordering. The migration is automatic, keeps all your data, and requires nothing from you.
  • Improved Security: the sign-in callback is now validated against the complete configured redirect address rather than just parts of it.
  • Fixed Reliability: transaction updates no longer send the date field, which YNAB fixes at creation and ignores afterward.
v1.1.2 July 24, 2026

One screen to scan, edit, and upload - with live YNAB status on every receipt.

  • Improved Scanning and editing unified: reviewing a fresh scan and editing a saved receipt now use one consistent screen and set of rules.
  • Improved Save, then upload: after saving a scan, the upload button appears in the same screen. No more hunting for the receipt in the list.
  • New YNAB status on every receipt: uploaded, matched to your bank, not found, or on this device only - with the target account always visible.
  • Improved Smarter sync: syncing brings back the current payee, account, and category from YNAB. If a transaction was deleted in YNAB, the receipt returns to "on this device only" and offers Check YNAB Again.
  • Improved Duplicate protection: one tap creates exactly one transaction, even if the connection drops mid-upload.
  • Improved Accessibility: fully readable amounts at the largest Dynamic Type sizes, improved VoiceOver announcements.
  • Improved Reliability: many improvements under the hood.
v1.1.1 July 17, 2026

In-app data deletion.

  • New In-app data deletion: delete everything the app stores, right from the app. Settings > YNAB Account > Data removes all local data (receipts, scans, extracted line items) and disconnects your YNAB account.
  • New Precision matters: this deletes local app data only. Anything already synced to YNAB stays in your budget untouched.
  • New Deletion is literal: since all processing is on-device and receipt images never leave your iPhone, there is no server copy to delete, because there never was one.
v1.1.0 May 27, 2026

Private Receipt Links — find a receipt from any YNAB transaction without uploading the photo.

  • New Private Receipt Links — Each receipt you sync now drops a small reference code (e.g. [RFY:20260511183022]) into the YNAB transaction's memo. Copy the code from YNAB, paste it on the home screen, and the matching receipt opens instantly. The photo stays on your iPhone the whole time.
  • New Receipt detail footer — Synced receipts show a copyable code at the bottom of the detail view.
  • New Backfill for existing receipts — A one-time action in Settings → YNAB Account adds link codes to receipts you synced under earlier versions.
  • Improved Pricing — Launch special retired. AI Scanning monthly is now $2.99/month (previously $4.99). Yearly stays at $29.99/year.

Better scanning for long receipts and stability improvements.

  • Improved YNAB merchant names — Long merchant names are now handled gracefully when syncing to YNAB
  • Fixed Upgrade stability — Resolved a crash that could occur when updating from older versions of the app
  • Improved Scan cancellation — Cancelling a scan mid-process is now faster and more reliable
v1.0.8 March 17, 2026

Fractional quantities, gas station receipts, and scanning accuracy improvements.

  • New Fractional quantities — Enter weighted items like 0.335 kg of produce. Quantities are no longer limited to whole numbers
  • New Gas station receipts — Dedicated detection for fuel receipts. Correctly extracts fuel grade, total, and ignores volume/unit price
  • Improved Price extraction accuracy — Fixed currency values being split across lines (e.g., "$85." and "50" now correctly read as $85.50)
  • Improved Quantity accuracy — The AI no longer fabricates quantities by merging same-named items. Each receipt line is extracted individually
  • Fixed Upload to YNAB button — Redesigned toolbar for easier access. Now always visible for local receipts, with a helpful prompt if no account is selected
  • Improved Accessibility — Better VoiceOver grouping for line items, improved notification announcements, and Reduce Motion support throughout
